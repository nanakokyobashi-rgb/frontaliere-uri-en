Company Description

A++ Gruppo is looking for a Front Office professional to join its team at the new location in Andermatt, Canton Uri.

Position

Front Office

Requirements

Main responsibilities

Operational management of the property

  • Oversee the property's daily operations, ensuring order, functionality and compliance with company standards.
  • Manage check-in and check-out procedures, ensuring a professional, courteous welcome focused on guest satisfaction.
  • Coordinate suppliers, tradespeople and maintenance work, ensuring timeliness, efficiency and service quality.
  • Provide advice and suggestions on local attractions, restaurants, events, activities and local experiences.
  • Ensure a professional presence during the established hours, handling guests' requests and needs discreetly and promptly.
  • Carry out tidying and preparation of rooms and common areas, both before arrival and during guests' stay.
  • Ensure high standards of cleanliness, order and attention to detail, in line with company standards.
  • Supervise and verify the Housekeeping service, ensuring the proper preparation of the premises and service quality.

Profile

  • Proven experience in the role, preferably gained in the Hospitality sector.
  • Excellent command of English.
  • Good knowledge of German.
  • Availability to work shifts, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Strong guest orientation, professionalism, flexibility and attention to detail.

Other information

We offer

  • A full-time permanent contract in a professional and dynamic environment.
  • Salary commensurate with the candidate’s experience and skills.
