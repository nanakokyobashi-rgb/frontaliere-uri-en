Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Professional management and continuous optimization of disposal and recycling processes, including analysis of waste and material flows
  • Planning, management and coordination of operational processes, orders, projects as well as liquidation and non-service contracts (operational + administrative)
  • Procurement management: procurement, evaluation, ordering, procurement of materials and services and control of material inputs (electronics)
  • Definition, marketing and management of fractions (metals, plastics, scrap) and customer, supplier and government relations
  • Leadership, instruction and support of the team, ensuring environmental, safety and legal requirements as well as producing reports and documentation

You bring that with you

  • Completed technical training with further education, e.g. technical merchant or dipl. Logistician/HF
  • mandatory several years of professional experience in the waste disposal and recycling industry as well as in logistics
  • Professional and leadership experience as well as strong team and communication skills
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, negotiating skills and high service orientation
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Good knowledge of the legal framework in waste management
  • Existing network in the disposal and recycling industry is an advantage
  • Ability to use and further develop existing contacts specifically for operational and strategic issues
  • Very good knowledge of German, English and/or French of advantage, SAP knowledge of advantage

Don't you meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
