Full description

Our hospital is a central component of healthcare in the region. With a broad range of services, we ensure an outpatient and inpatient extended basic care and offer our patients high-quality medical and nursing care. As one of the largest employers in the region, we attach great importance to innovation, quality and a modern working environment. The ICT department plays a key role in ensuring the smooth operation and digitalisation of the hospital. You are responsible for the maintenance and further development of the existing IT infrastructure, including the clinic information system. With modern IT infrastructures as well as our new building and conversions, which were completed in 2022 and 2024, we offer you an innovative and future-oriented working environment. You can actively shape the digital transformation. You shape IT governance and IT security and play a key role in the development of the digitalisation, CIS, AI and IT strategy. We are looking for a responsible, dutiful and independent personality as head of ICT. Technology has long since become more than infrastructure in the hospital – it is a prerequisite for safe medicine, reliable care and modern patient care. For our hospital, we are looking for a personality who not only runs IT, but shapes the future: In ICT, you take responsibility for an efficient digital basis, for strategic decisions and for solutions that significantly improve the everyday life of employees and patients. The function works closely with the top management level (C-level) on strategic issues.

  • You ensure that ICT works reliably in 24/7 hospital operations – even when it is particularly important.
  • You support the hospital council and the hospital management in the further development and implementation of the digitalization strategy.
  • You develop the IT strategy further and make it understandable, sustainable and feasible for the organization.
  • You design a responsible AI strategy that enables innovation while taking into account security, privacy, ethics and regulatory requirements.
  • You shape the further development of the clinic information system and create digital foundations for efficient, safe and patient-oriented processes.
  • You support the hospital management and the departments in identifying, prioritizing and using digital opportunities through appropriate measures.
  • You lead the ICT organization with clarity, appreciation and future orientation.
  • You strengthen information security, cyber resilience, data protection and business continuity as the central basis of trust of the hospital.
  • You control budgets, investments, suppliers and resources with an entrepreneurial eye and a high sense of responsibility. In this key role, you are part of the strategic leadership and bring the ICT perspective to where important decisions for the further development of the hospital are made. You combine technological excellence with a deep understanding of the needs of medicine, care, operations and administration. In doing so, you create trust, provide orientation and ensure that digitization does not remain an end in itself, but creates real benefits in everyday care.
  • You bring a sound tertiary education in computer science, business informatics, medical informatics or a comparable qualification.
  • You have already successfully led ICT organizations – ideally in healthcare or in a demanding, regulated 24/7 environment.
  • You know the requirements of a stable ICT operation as well as the challenges of strategic transformation.
  • You understand clinical processes, clinic information systems, interfaces and data flows – or you have the curiosity and experience to quickly and deeply familiarize yourself with these topics.
  • You think strategically, act pragmatically and realize where digitalization, data and AI can create real added value.
  • You communicate clearly, lead firmly and remain calm, solution-oriented and reliable even in demanding situations. Your perspectives
  • A versatile and meaningful working environment in a modern hospital operation.
  • Modern IT, medical and building technology solutions as well as exciting projects in a future-oriented infrastructure.
  • The opportunity to actively participate in the further development of modern ICT management and the digital transformation of the hospital.
  • A motivated team that values cooperation, personal responsibility and mutual support.
  • Attractive employment conditions, further education opportunities and a targeted promotion of your professional development.
