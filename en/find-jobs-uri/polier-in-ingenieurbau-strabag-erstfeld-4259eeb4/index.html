Full description

At STRABAG, around 89,000 people at more than 2,400 locations worldwide are building progress. Uniqueness and individual strengths characterise not only our projects but also each and every one of us. Whether in building and civil engineering, road and underground construction, bridge and tunnel construction, in building materials production, project development or building management – we think construction forward to become the most innovative and sustainable construction technology group in Europe. Equal opportunities, diversity and inclusion are integral parts of what defines us as a company and how we work. Together we implement projects successfully and in partnership and grow with new tasks. Together we create great things. Let's progress!

Foreman: Civil Engineering Your contribution with us

  • You lead the teams on our construction sites
  • You implement technical specifications and construction plans precisely in practice
  • You are responsible for quality, safety and compliance with technical standards
  • You coordinate specialist trades and subcontractors
  • You manage the use of materials and equipment on the construction site
  • You support billing, documentation and technical queries

What counts for us

  • Completed training as a bricklayer EFZ with further training to foreman or site manager
  • Several years of experience as a foreman or site supervisor in building, road or civil engineering is an advantage
  • You work independently, committed and reliably
  • You lead cooperatively and enjoy teamwork
  • You are a doer and push things forward on the construction site

Our added value for you

  • Projects you can be proud of
  • A professional construction company that has been committed to the Uri location for years and constantly develops it further
  • A young, well-coordinated team with genuine cohesion
  • Cool company events such as our Christmas dinner or internal football tournament
  • State-of-the-art machines and equipment that make work fun
  • Short decision-making paths and real scope for design
  • Fair employment conditions and training that really advance you
