Project Manager / Charge Infrastructure Specialist CC (M/F) 80–100%

In the field of electric mobility, we are looking for a specialist with an entrepreneurial spirit in the Swiss charging infrastructure department, mainly in French-speaking Switzerland, now or now.

Your mission

  • Operational piloting of installations and technical monitoring, from preparation to commissioning, at the heart of our CC charging infrastructure projects
  • Senior Interlocutor for clients, partners, suppliers and internal teams throughout the project
  • Tendering for direct requests and technical assistance for invitations to tender
  • Development of installation concepts, cable paths and materialization
  • Planning and command of internal and external resources
  • Monitoring and advice on the implementation and implementation of safety and quality guidelines
  • Ensure regular presence on construction sites to monitor progress and ensure compliance with safety, quality and time requirements
  • Independent search of breakdowns, assistance and support of service organisation
  • Availability for ad hoc interventions
  • Interventions throughout Switzerland, mainly in French-speaking Switzerland

Your profile

  • SWC of installer-electrician, building technician, automatician, Electrical Project Leader in Installation and Safety (BF), Electrical Engineering Engineer or any other training deemed equivalent
  • Experience in managing and monitoring technical projects, ideally in charging infrastructure, electromobility or electrical installations
  • At ease both in the field and with coordination and planning activities
  • Mastery of French and good knowledge of German or English
  • Good IT skills and ease with digital tools
  • Proactive, autonomous, pragmatic and solutions-oriented personality
  • Ideally located in the Bienne, Fribourg, Neuchâtel or Yverdon region

What we offer you

  • A key role in a growing sector linked to the energy transition.
  • Concrete and visible projects that contribute to the development of electric mobility.
  • Wide autonomy and responsibilities between field and project management
  • Engaged teams and strong collaboration among specialists
  • Modern working tools and flexible working conditions
  • Continuing training opportunities and prospects for change
  • The opportunity to participate actively in the development of our charging infrastructure activity
