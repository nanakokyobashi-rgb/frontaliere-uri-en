Full description

With its new building and state-of-the-art infrastructure, the cantonal hospital Uri ensures extended, outpatient and stationary basic care for the population of the canton Uri. For our catering team we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as an employee as of 1 February 2027 or by appointment: in dishwashing room. YOUR TASKS

  • Handling and cleaning of dishwashers and of kitchen utensils and kitchen equipment
  • Ensuring hygiene rules and implementing the internal hygiene concept
  • Ensuring the supply of dishes, cutlery and glasses in the restaurant area
  • Return transport and cleaning of dirt utensils and maintenance of the wagon park
  • Proper handling of cleaning products and correct waste management
  • Assistance on the sidewalk during service hours and support during set-up work YOUR PROFIL
  • First practical experience in gastronomy, or a certificate "Perfecto" or "Progresso" is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German (at least level B1/B2)
  • Knowledge of hygiene, cleaning and handling of dishwashers and cleaning products
  • Physical resilience, reliability and an independent, careful working method
  • Teamwork, flexibility and enjoyment of collaboration
  • Friendly appearance and strong service orientation
  • High quality and hygiene awareness of your personalities
  • Working in a modern infrastructure
  • Attractive employee offers and diverse activities
  • Varied and responsible work
  • Working in a motivated and experienced team
  • Structured familiarization and appreciative working environment
