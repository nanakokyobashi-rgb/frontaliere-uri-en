Full description

Tasks

  • Accompanying, consulting and taking over psychotherapeutic treatment of children and adolescents, including family members
  • clarification and therapies of patients with different types of disorders
  • networking with other specialist centres (school psychology service, paediatricians, children's and youth, psychotherapy, school social work, authorities)
  • Creating treatment documentation, writing of specialist reports and conducting administrative work
  • Possibility of conducting group therapies

Requirements

  • University degree in psychology and completed or advanced further education in psychotherapy
  • professional experience in the children's and jibends' environments and in therapeutic work with families
  • High Aness and interest in social jibends' and psychotherapeutic issues
  • Empathic handling of patients and commitment to individual needs
  • High independence, personal responsibility and proven social competence
