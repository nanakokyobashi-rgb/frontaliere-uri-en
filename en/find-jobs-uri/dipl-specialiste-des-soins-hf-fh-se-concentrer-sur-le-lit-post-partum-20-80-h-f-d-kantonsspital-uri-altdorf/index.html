Full description

With its comprehensive range of services, our hospital with modern infrastructure ensures the extended outpatient and stationary basic care for the canton of Uri. On our interdisciplinary acute care unit, we look after patients from different disciplines. A particular focus is on the care of women in the postpartum and gynecological patients. We attach great importance to competent, personal and sensitive care and a holistic accompaniment of mother and child. We live an active DU culture with flat hierarchies and promote an open, collegial working environment. You can expect a versatile environment with attractive learning and development opportunities. Immediately or by agreement we are looking for an interested and committed Dipl. Care specialist HF/FH postpartum bed 20–80%. YOUR TASKS

  • Competent and holistic care of women and baby in postpartum as well as gynecological patients
  • Accompaniment of mothers and families during the inpatient stay and support in their new role
  • Advice and guidance around breastfeeding, infant care, postpartum and postpartum
  • Co-creation and continuous further development of the quality of care, the processes in the postpartum bed as well as in the new projects of outpatient breastfeeding advice and midwife-guided obstetrics YOUR PROFIL
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH with professional experience in the acute hospital, ideally with experience in the field of postpartum/KJFF
  • Interest and joy in caring for women and families around birth
  • Sensitive, helpful and communicative personality
  • Responsible, independent and committed working methods
  • Willingness to take on additional services in the event of increased workload OUR OFFER
  • A versatile and responsible activity with a focus on postpartum bed in a hospital with a state-of-the-art infrastructure
  • A varied working environment with close contact to women, newborns and families, in a motivated and well-coordinated team
  • Attractive employment conditions with extensive social benefits
  • A dynamic environment in which you can contribute your expertise and further develop our head of acute care, Bettina Imholz-Rieder, will be happy to provide you with further information on 041 875 53 54. We look forward to your application.
